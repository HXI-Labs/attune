# Archived STARSS23 model-search direction

The existing STARSS23 scripts, manifests, checkpoints references, and reports
remain in their original paths so historical links and experiment hashes do not
break. They are archived logically rather than deleted.

The active v0.1 route does not continue decoder grids, tiled MLPs, GRUs, or the
single Conv1D retry. The best recorded natural-scene collar F1 remains 0.1395,
below the fixed 0.25 wiring gate. STARSS23 is retained as source-labelled
natural-scene evaluation only. DCASE remains the strong-timing training source,
and neither corpus is promoted to Attune gold.
