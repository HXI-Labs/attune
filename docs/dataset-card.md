# Dataset card template

Complete one card and provenance ledger per dataset before ingestion.

## Identity and purpose

- Dataset/version:
- Source and stable URL:
- Intended Attune task:
- Maintainer/contact:
- Collection dates and geography:

## Rights and consent

- Dataset licence:
- Audio redistribution permitted:
- Derivative annotations permitted:
- Commercial/research restrictions:
- Consent and participant expectations:
- Licence review owner, date, and status:

`pending` licence status blocks training and redistribution.

## Contents and annotation

Document languages, hours/items, speaker counts, recording conditions,
demographic fields (only if justified), transcription source, annotation
protocol and ontology version, annotator population, agreement, soft-label
retention, missing data, and known offensive/sensitive content.

## Splits and leakage

Splits must be speaker-disjoint. Record speaker/session/episode grouping,
duplicate detection, contamination checks, and immutable manifest hashes.

## Risks and limitations

Describe representation gaps, consent risks, label subjectivity, likely
recording and subgroup biases, privacy handling, retention/deletion rules, and
prohibited uses. Link the exact `data/provenance/*.yaml` ledger.
