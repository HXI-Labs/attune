# Attune v0.1 public-data protocol

## Claim boundary

v0.1 is a technical research release trained and evaluated with public source
annotations. It may report performance against those annotations, but it may
not call them Attune gold, infer internal emotion, or claim that human users
prefer downstream responses. A future human study is specified separately.

## Active evidence

| Branch | Training/development evidence | Sealed evidence | Output boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| ASR | bounded Common Voice English replay | British Common Voice and NC Ghana inspection | transcript and genuine CTC word timing |
| Events | DCASE strong synthetic timing; VocalSound/FSD isolated clips | DCASE public test and source-labelled STARSS23 | localized spans; natural and synthetic results separate |
| Styles | licence-filtered FSD shout/whisper clips | held-out source clips | utterance scope only |
| Affect | CREMA-D paired acted speech; MSP-Podcast after access | speaker/corpus-disjoint source labels | categorical distribution plus abstention |
| V/A/D | MSP-Podcast after access | official held-out partitions | unavailable until licensed data exists |

`crying_speech`, `laughing_speech`, `singing`, `strained_speech`, and `breath`
remain in the broader ontology but are not emitted by the v0.1 trained model.

## Split and leakage rules

- Preserve official tests; never use them for thresholds, early stopping, OOD
  fitting, or quantization calibration.
- Keep speakers, source recordings, rooms, and sessions in one partition.
- Store every audio/feature SHA-256 and the source licence record.
- Missing task labels are masked. They are never converted into negative
  examples merely because another corpus lacks that annotation.
- CREMA same-sentence groups receive stable pair IDs. Conflict rows may carry a
  lexical affect control label solely for APS evaluation.
- Ghana audio remains NC research-only and never enters training or a
  redistributable artifact.

## Candidate sequence

1. Train new heads with the complete SenseVoice backbone frozen.
2. Train a separate candidate with only the upper two encoder blocks unfrozen.
3. Select using development-only WER preservation, frame/localization quality,
   affect/VAD quality, calibration, APS, and runtime.
4. Export only the selected FP candidate, fit runtime calibration, quantize to
   INT8, recalibrate, and evaluate FP/INT8 exactly once on sealed partitions.

Do not continue an expensive candidate if its development ASR exceeds the base
by more than one absolute WER point or it cannot beat/match the frozen probe on
its labelled tasks.

## Release gates

- Fewer than 300M total parameters.
- FP WER no more than one absolute point worse than base SenseVoice.
- Positive APS on declared semantic/acoustic conflict rows.
- Selective risk improves as coverage falls.
- INT8 WER loss at most 0.5 absolute point and event/affect macro-F1 loss at
  most two absolute points.
- Schema and XML validity 100%; no transcript-to-metadata injection path.
- M1/8 GB RTF below 1.0, with 0.6 preferred.
- All metrics identify source-label status and separate synthetic timing from
  natural-scene evidence.
